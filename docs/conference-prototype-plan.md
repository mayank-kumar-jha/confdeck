# Conference prototype plan

The existing Sennheiser/OmniTrack camera tracking path remains the owner of camera presets. Conference inputs are optional and configured from Display → Conference inputs and far-end protection.

1. Confirm a USB input carries **only** the VC system's playback to the room speakers. A mixed microphone and playback signal is unsuitable. Compare silence and remote speech with Test input level, then set channel and threshold.
2. Enable far-end protection. A loud input pauses new camera triggers; the hold time prevents rapid toggling. Missing or stale audio frames pause triggers rather than allowing unintended camera moves. Disable the feature to return to the existing behavior. An activity threshold cannot distinguish remote speech from music or other audio on this channel, and simultaneous local speech is intentionally suppressed.
3. For the requested laptop conferencing setup, connect the mini PC's HDMI **output** to the HDMI **input** of a compatible USB capture device, then connect the capture device's USB plug to the laptop. Show the existing camera viewer on the mini PC HDMI output and select the capture device as the camera in the VC application. A TV is not required. Confirm the capture device's port labels, HDMI output and video format before testing; a direct HDMI connection between laptop and mini PC outputs cannot provide the return.
4. Route the VC laptop's speaker playback to the DSP and provide the mini PC with a separate far-end-only audio input or activity signal. The HDMI capture USB video connection alone does not establish that return audio path. Verify both routing and camera preview in a live call.
5. Configure the microphone's LocalOut audio source for speech validation. After reset, the previous AES67 source is deliberately blocked pending setup; do not silently reuse an old source.
6. Run a labeled room test: local only, far end only, switching, silence/noise, disconnected USB, lost capture signal, and simultaneous speech. Tune the input threshold and hold time and verify presets and meeting audio. The first version does not support local tracking during simultaneous speech.
7. Investigate microphone LED integration separately, preserving mute/status indications and validating firmware behavior. The current prototype exposes blue/green state in the display controls but does not change the physical mic LEDs.
